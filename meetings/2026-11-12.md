# Notes

## ZTRUST Working Group

Rick Troth presented the annual review for the ZTRUST Working Group.

## Linux Distributions Working Group

Sarah Julia Kriesch and Elizabeth Joseph presented the annual review for the Linux Distributions Working Group.

## TAC Vice Chair Election

John Mertic reported that Mike Friesenegger is stepping down as chairperson at year end, and Mark Bylok will assume that role for 2027\. Mertic added that nominations are open for vice chair and voting members should reach out to Mertic or Tom Slanda if they are interested.

## Green Screen Séance Infrastructure Request

Mertic reviewed the Green Screen Séance infrastructure request, which is a hackathon project proposing tooling that connects 3270 applications to MCP. Discussion ensued, and the representatives agreed that the project is probably early-stage and an upstream collaboration with Zowe MCP may be the better path.
