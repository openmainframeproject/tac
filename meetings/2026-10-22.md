# Notes

## COBOL Working Group Review

Jeff Brown reported that the remaining members of the working group have recommended a pause.  Tom Slanda will create a poll for voting members in LFX.

## Feilong Annual Review

Mike Friesenegger presented the Feilong Annual Review.

## Review/renew TAC Industry Representatives for 2027

John Mertic reviewed the requirements for the TAC Industry Representative and said the nomination period will open in November 2026\.

## Cloudflare containers for projects

Mertic reported that Cloudflare containers will be made available to Open Mainframe Project projects in 2027, as a part of the project’s contract with The Linux Foundation’s Release Engineering team.

## Help with reviewing non-OMP project entries in the landscape

Mertic requested assistance in updating the Open Mainframe Project landscape, specifically the projects that are outside of the Open Mainframe Project.

## TAC Strategy Planning for 2027

Slanda stated that an upcoming November TAC meeting will focus on setting 2-3 goals for 2027\. Slanda added that TAC Members should begin considering their vision and priorities for the TAC in the meantime.
